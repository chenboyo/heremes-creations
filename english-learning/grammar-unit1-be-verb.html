🧙‍♂️ Be 動詞三俠的故事

從前有三個魔法師，他們各自認不同的人。點擊卡片聽發音！

🧙
am
只認「I」
I am a student.
🧙‍♂️
is
認 he / she / it
He is tall.
🧙‍♀️
are
認 you / we / they
We are friends.
📋 記憶口訣 & 規則表
記憶口訣：
「I 用 am，he/she/it 用 is」
「複數（2人以上）統統用 are」
主詞Be 動詞例句✓ / ✗
IamI am a student.✅
YouareYou are cool.✅
He / She / ItisShe is pretty.✅
We / TheyareThey are here.✅
Tom (名詞)isTom is a teacher.✅
IareI are a student.❌
SheareShe are nice.❌
⚠️ 常見地雷
❌ I are a student.
→ am 只認 I，I 只能配 am！
❌ She are nice.
→ she 是單數，要配 is！
❌ They is happy.
→ they 是複數，要配 are！
