💭 家族一：抽象概念

🌾 家族二：顆粒粉末
🍚 rice 🧂 salt ⬜ sugar 🌾 flour 🥬 pepper
💧 家族三：液體氣體
💧 water 🥛 milk ☕ coffee 🌬️ air 🍵 tea 🧴 juice
⚠️ 可數 vs 不可數陷阱
❌ a water → ✅ a glass of water
water 不可數，需要容器單位
❌ an informations → ✅ a piece of information
information 不可數，不能加 s
❌ a rice → ✅ a grain / a bowl of rice
rice 整體不可數，米粒才可以數
❌ three breads → ✅ three pieces of bread
bread 是物質名詞，不能直接加 s
📏 實用單位片語
單位搭配例句
a cup ofcoffee, tea☕ a cup of coffee
a glass ofwater, juice💧 a glass of water
a bottle ofwater, juice🧴 a bottle of water
a slice ofbread, cheese🍞 a slice of bread
a piece ofcheese, information🧀 a piece of cheese
a bowl ofrice🍚 a bowl of rice
a grain ofrice, salt🌾 a grain of rice
